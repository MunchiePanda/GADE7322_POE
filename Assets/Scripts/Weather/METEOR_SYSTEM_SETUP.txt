PROCEDURAL METEOR SYSTEM - SETUP GUIDE
======================================

OVERVIEW:
---------
The new Procedural Meteor System creates visually varied meteors with procedurally generated shapes using vertex displacement.
Meteor danger is visually correlated with size, shape displacement, and color intensity.
Special defenders (Frost/Lightning) are no longer instant-killed and take reduced damage.
Healing meteors can spawn to restore defender health.

FEATURES:
---------
1. Procedural Meteor Shapes: Uses vertex displacement shader to create unique jagged/rough meteor shapes
2. Size-Danger Correlation: Larger meteors = more displacement, brighter colors, more damage
3. Special Defender Protection: Frost and Lightning towers take 60% damage (configurable)
4. Healing Meteors: Small meteors (0.3-0.8 size range) have a chance to heal defenders instead of damage
5. Visual Feedback: Color-coded warnings (orange for damage, green for healing)
6. No Rain/Earthquake: System focuses exclusively on meteors

SETUP INSTRUCTIONS:
-------------------

METHOD 1: Use New Procedural Meteor System (Recommended)
---------------------------------------------------------
1. Add the "ProceduralMeteorSystem" component to your WeatherController GameObject
2. In WeatherController inspector:
   - Assign the ProceduralMeteorSystem reference to the "Procedural Meteor System" field
3. Configure ProceduralMeteorSystem settings:
   - Meteor Spawn Settings: Control number of meteors per wave (2-8 default)
   - Damage Settings: Base damage and special defender multiplier (0.6 = 60% damage)
   - Healing Meteor Settings: Enable/disable healing meteors, size range (0.3-0.8), healing amount
   - Procedural Shape Settings: Displacement amount and frequency (affects visual roughness)
4. The shader "Custom/ProceduralMeteorShader" will be automatically used

METHOD 2: Keep Using Old WeatherController
-------------------------------------------
The old meteor system in WeatherController still works but without:
- Procedural vertex displacement shapes
- Healing meteors
- Special defender damage reduction
- Size-based visual correlation

CUSTOMIZATION:
--------------

Meteor Danger Levels:
- Small meteors (0.5-1.0): Low displacement, dim colors, low damage
- Medium meteors (1.0-2.0): Medium displacement, bright colors, medium damage
- Large meteors (2.0-3.0): High displacement (rough/jagged), very bright, high damage

Healing Meteors:
- Size range: 0.3-0.8 (configurable)
- Spawn chance: 20% for meteors in size range
- Visual: Green color with upward-floating particles
- Effect: Restores 25 HP to defenders in impact radius

Special Defender Protection:
- FrostTowerDefender: Takes 60% damage from meteors (no instant-kill)
- LightningTowerDefender: Takes 60% damage from meteors (no instant-kill)
- Adjust "Special Defender Damage Multiplier" to change protection level

SHADER PARAMETERS:
------------------
The ProceduralMeteorShader creates varied shapes automatically.
Parameters (set procedurally, no manual adjustment needed):
- Displacement Amount: 0.05-0.5 (small to large meteors)
- Displacement Frequency: 4-12 (smoothness of surface)
- Displacement Seed: Random per meteor (ensures unique shapes)

TESTING:
--------
1. Enter Play Mode
2. Start a wave to trigger meteor strike
3. Observe:
   - Orange warning cylinders for damage meteors
   - Green warning cylinders for healing meteors (if enabled)
   - Varied meteor shapes (jagged/rough vs smooth)
   - Brighter/larger meteors deal more damage
   - Special defenders survive meteor impacts
   - Green particles on healing impact

TROUBLESHOOTING:
----------------
- Purple/pink meteors: Shader not found. Check "Custom/ProceduralMeteorShader" exists
- No shape variation: Displacement settings too low. Increase Max Displacement
- Special defenders still die: Check damage multiplier is 0.6 or lower
- No healing meteors: Enable "Enable Healing Meteors" checkbox
- Meteors spawn at origin: Assign VoxelTerrainGenerator reference

INTEGRATION NOTES:
------------------
- The new system works alongside the existing WeatherController
- Rain and earthquake systems have been disabled in WeatherController
- Old meteor system remains as fallback if ProceduralMeteorSystem is not assigned
- Both systems can coexist but ProceduralMeteorSystem is recommended

For questions or issues, check the component tooltips or review the script comments.
